Almost nobody fully understands how modern AI works — not even the people building
it. That makes it easy to fear, easy to hype, and hard to think clearly about.

I started this to fix that for myself — then realized it might be just as useful to
anyone else trying to make sense of AI. It begins as a **glossary** — short,
plain-language definitions of the terms I kept tripping over while running models on
my own machine, explained the way you'd explain them to a friend. From
there it grows into **practice** notes, **blog** posts, and **deep dives** into the
machinery underneath. No hype, no hand-waving — because you can't have a sane
opinion about something you don't understand.
