# mascot

The site has a quiet recurring mascot: a **small shih tzu**. She shows up in the
site's illustrations from time to time - asleep in a corner of a scene, sitting
near the work, never the subject. She is a tribute, kept deliberately low-key.

## The one hard rule

**She is never named.** Not in site copy, alt text, captions, filenames, image
prompts, notes.md entries, commit messages, or this file. This repo is public;
her identity is private on purpose and lives only in Claude's project memory.
In anything repo-visible she is "the shih tzu" or "the little dog."

## When she appears

- **Sparingly.** "From time to time" is the brief - a recurring presence, not a
  watermark. Eric picks or approves each placement; don't seed her into every
  image unprompted.
- She suits quiet domestic corners of a scene: workshops, desks, print shops,
  reading rooms. She replaces the incidental-cat slot in that kind of scene.
- She belongs in the art, not the prose. The text never points her out.

## How to draw her (prompt language)

Add to a concept brief, matching the scene's grammar:

> a small shih tzu with a round face, short muzzle, long floppy ears, and a
> flowing floor-length coat, asleep on/curled up beside <something in the scene>

- Keep her small in frame and at rest; she is a companion detail, not a gag.
- Works in any of the house styles (see `concepts/README.md`); the usual "no
  text, no words" suffix applies.
