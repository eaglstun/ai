### The export form found the killswitch before the safety team did

- **The news:** Claude Fable 5 shipped June 9, 2026, then a U.S. export-control directive
  forced it offline within three days, triggered after Amazon's security team flagged a
  jailbreak to the White House. (https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/06/claude-5-release/)
- **Eric's angle:** The interesting part is the _legal instrument_. Nobody invented a new
  category to pull Fable 5. They reached for export control, the same machinery built to
  stop you mailing a missile guidance chip overseas. A frontier model got governed like
  munitions the first time it scared someone, which is the whole thesis: AI's first real law
  was a weapons law, written before anyone agreed it was a weapon. The wrinkle that makes it
  honest, not smug: it wasn't pulled over some abstract doomsday capability, it was a
  jailbreak. The munitions framework got invoked over a bug.

  safety-patch angle) or a glossary term. Flagging for Eric: reality just proved the 404
  right, and the joke is now that the proof points at a 404. That meta-move is yours to make
  or leave.
- **Route:** blog, or a Threads reaction while it is live news.

### Ted Chiang says the magician has no rabbit, and he is right and it does not matter

- **The news:** Ted Chiang (The Atlantic, ~June 4, 2026) argues LLM conversations are
  sophisticated sentence continuation, and singles out Anthropic for anthropomorphizing
  Claude as a being with values, intentions, even "anxiety."
  (https://aitoolly.com/ai-news/article/2026-06-04-ted-chiang-rejects-ai-consciousness-a-critique-of-anthropics-anthropomorphism-and-the-risks-of-mispl)
- **Eric's angle:** Chiang is correct about the mechanism and that is exactly why the critique
  undersells the thing. "I go to magicians to be surprised, not to learn the trick." Knowing
  it is sentence continuation does not make the surprise fake, it makes it cheaper to produce,
  which is stranger. And Anthropic is the one most exposed here precisely because it is the
  lab that talks most about Claude's inner life. A fair, principled poke at Anthropic: you
  cannot market a conscience and then hide behind "it is just autocomplete" when someone
  audits the claim. Pick one.
- **The click:** /blog/you-cant-get-to-a-mind-one-bead-at-a-time/ and
  /blog/a-conscience-you-can-patch-out-overnight/ both already argue this terrain. The
  glossary term /glossary/agi/ is the cold-water landing for a reader who wants the plain
  version.
- **Route:** blog. This is a centerpiece, not a reply.
- **Freshness:** mostly evergreen, with a 2 to 3 week timeliness tail while the Chiang piece
  is being passed around.

### MLX quietly ate llama.cpp's lunch and Ollama just admitted it

- **The news:** As of mid-2026, MLX is reported to beat llama.cpp by 30 to 40% on M5
  hardware, and Ollama 0.19+ now runs MLX under the hood on Apple Silicon.
  (https://apxml.com/posts/best-local-llms-apple-silicon-mac)
- **Eric's angle:** This is the home-lab beat with a clean before/after. For a year the
  advice was "Ollama for convenience, MLX for speed, pick your tradeoff." That tradeoff just
  collapsed: the convenient one is now wearing the fast one as a skin. The plain-language
  payoff is that unified memory plus a native framework stopped being a hobbyist footnote and
  became the default, and a 64GB Mac now runs models a 24GB 4090 simply cannot hold. The
  agent did the porting grunt-work on the case studies, the lesson is the architecture.
- **The click:** built-in. /glossary/mlx/ and /glossary/llamacpp-vs-ollama/ are now suddenly
  concrete, and the Apple-Silicon deep-dives (porting-ml-to-apple-silicon,
  ctranslate2-metal-backend) are the proof-of-work.
- **Route:** practice, or a deep-dive update. Possibly a glossary refresh on
  llamacpp-vs-ollama since the answer materially changed.
- **Freshness:** timely with WWDC 2026 (June 8 to 12) in the air, but the underlying point
  ages well.

### The bill came due, right on the schedule I posted

- **The news:** June 2026 is the month metered AI billing arrived at once: GitHub Copilot
  moved from request-based to usage-based billing citing unsustainable inference costs, and
  Anthropic split subscription programmatic usage into a separate credit pool starting June 15. (https://llm-stats.com/ai-news ,
  https://devtoolpicks.com/blog/anthropic-splits-claude-subscriptions-agent-sdk-credit-june-2026)
- **Eric's angle:** The all-you-can-eat era is closing in the same quarter, across vendors,
  for the same reason: the unit economics never worked, they were just venture-subsidized.
  This is the de-evolution motif in its natural habitat. We are watching a product category
  evolve backward, from "unlimited magic" to "the meter is running," because the magic was
  always being billed to someone else. The honest self-implicating version: I have a post
  about a 150-dollar afternoon. That afternoon is about to be everyone's pricing page.
- **The click:** /blog/the-bill-comes-due/ is named for this exact moment, and
  /blog/three-hours-and-150-dollars/ is the receipt. Pairing them under a fresh news peg is a
  strong internal-link move.
- **Route:** blog, or a Threads post pointing at the-bill-comes-due.
- **Freshness:** react now while both billing changes are this-week news.
